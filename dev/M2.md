### 1.2 The key lemma [Proved]
**`perAn_inj`.** Let `N ≥ 2`, let `b` be adapted (`θ(j, e) = ζ^e b_j`, `e < φ(N)`, ℚ-independent), and assume
`∀ s, gcd(κ_s, N) = 1` and `P̄ ≠ 0`. If `q ∈ span_ℚ perAngen` and `q.2 = 0`, then `q = 0`.

*Proof.*
1. **Polynomial form** (the `perA_poly` induction). There are `f_j ∈ ℚ[X]` with `q.1(s, j) = P_s f_j(ζ^{κ_s})` and
   `q.2 = P̄ Σ_j b_j f_j(ζ^{N−1})`. Both components use the same `f_j`: the generator `(j, e)` has `f_j = X^e`, and
   all other `f` are 0.
2. **Change of root.** `q.2 = 0` and `P̄ ≠ 0` give `Σ_j b_j g_j(ζ) = 0`, with `g_j := f_j ∘ X^{N−1}`, because
   `f_j(ζ^{N−1}) = g_j(ζ)`.
3. **Independence.** Reduce `g_j` mod `Φ_N`, of degree `< φ(N)`. The ℚ-independence of `θ(j, e)` gives
   `g_j(ζ) = 0`, i.e. `f_j(ζ^{N−1}) = 0`. This is the step `hR`, `hzero`, `hdvd` of `perA_inj`, verbatim.
4. **Minimal polynomial.** `ζ^{N−1}` is a primitive `N`-th root, since `gcd(N − 1, N) = 1`. Its minimal polynomial is
   `Φ_N`, so `Φ_N | f_j`.
5. **Conclusion.** `f_j(ζ^{κ_s}) = 0` for every `s`, by `hκN`. So `q.1 = 0`, and `q = 0`. ∎

Lean shape: factor the core of `perA_inj` into a lemma
`sum_b_aeval_zero : Σ_j b_j · aeval ζ (f j) = 0 → ∀ j, Φ_N ∣ f j`. Then `perA_inj` and `perAn_inj` are 20–40 lines
each. Use `Polynomial.aeval_comp`, `IsPrimitiveRoot.pow_of_coprime` and `cyclotomic_eq_minpoly_rat`.

## 2. Step 2: architecture for `A^♮`

### 2.1 The group, and what the engine actually needs
* **The block** [Proved].
  - In the Baker model `Y² = 4X⁵ + 1`, the coordinates are `(z₀, z₁)`, dual to `dX/Y, X dX/Y`.
  - The ζ₅-eigenclasses of `H¹(A, O)` are `[X³dX/Y]` (char ζ⁴ = σ̄) and `[X²dX/Y]` (char ζ³). Here
    `dx/y = −5X³dX/Y` (`BetaPeriods` header).
  - `W′` contains the ζ³-fibre of every copy, so `Q″ = (A_σ̄^♮)^r / {Σ_j b_j w_j = 0}`, where `A_σ̄^♮` is the
    `G_a`-extension of `A` with class `[X³dX/Y]` (dimension 3).
  - (Side remark: `A_σ̄^♮` is also the generalized Jacobian `J_{2P}`, `P = (X, Y) = (0, −1)`, because
    `ω ↦ ω(P)` pairs with the ζ⁴-line. This is not used.)
* **Class shape.** `Q″` is VECEXT's class 𝒞 with `E_j ↦ A`, `r = 1`, `M = bᵀ` (onto), and `d₀ = 0`.
  - So VECEXT's `r ≤ 1` rationality repair is automatic: `Γ` is 0 or the fibre.
  - MEV's 4-block "gap" (`M` not onto, `D₂ < D₀`) does not arise.
  - MXP's `r = 2` dichotomy does not arise.
* **Every nonzero connected subgroup of `Q″` contains the fibre** [Proved].
  - `B ⊂ A^r` corresponds to an `F`-subspace `S`.
  - The fibre map of `B̃ → Q″` is `m ↦ Σ_j b_j σ̄(m_j)`, which is nonzero on `S ∖ 0` by the `F`-independence of `b`.
  - Hence `gmAn`'s `sub` holds for every member, and `G′ ∩ Q″ ∈ {0, fibre, B̃}`.
* **Periods only** [Proved].
  - Every `Q″`-part of `lam` is a period (`perAngen`), and the common-denominator trick applies
    (`hyperplaneKappaOn_of_scale`).
  - So lean_lsg's theorem may take `Λ(G)` with `Q″`-parts in `Ω(Q″)`. Then `exp_{Q″}` is the identity at every point
    of `Y(S)`.
  - Consequences: no `LiftAlg`, no algebraic points of `A^♮`, and Lemma U needs Taylor data **at the origin only**
    (as STAGE_A5 D2).
* **The engine group family.** The §5d engine needs Thm 4.1 for:

### 2.2 Analytic side (new file group `LSG/JacAnN/`)
Everything below is built from `θ = θ_N` (`JacAn3/Normalize`). Its factor is `facN ω z = facN ω 0 · e^{L_ω z}`
(`fac_affine`), and `L` is additive (`Lf_add`).

* **N1. Lemma K♮** [Proof + Spike]. For `i, j ∈ {1, 2}`:
  `∂_i∂_j log θ₀ + 3℘_ij(E z) ≡ c_ij` (constants), where `℘₁₁ = X₁/X₀`, `℘₁₂ = X₂/X₀`, `℘₂₂ = X₃/X₀`.
  - *Proof.* Let `H_ij := ∂_i(∂_jθ₀/θ₀) + 3θ_m/θ₀`. It is holomorphic on `{θ₀ ≠ 0}`.
    1. On `{θ_k ≠ 0}`, `k ∈ {4, 8}`: `H_ij = ∂_i∂_j log θ_k + Q_{k,ij}(θ)/θ_k²`, which is holomorphic. This follows
       from the chart-0 flow and one identity in `S₄/I₄` per `(k, i, j)`:
       `−(∂_i∂_jX_k)X_k + ∂_iX_k∂_jX_k + 3X_mX_k² ≡ Q_{k,ij}(X)` on the Mumford chart.
    2. Charts `{0, 4, 8}` cover (`theta_chart_cover`), so `H_ij` is entire.
    3. `H_ij` is Λ-periodic: `∂_j log θ₀` shifts by `L_ω(e_j)`, and `℘` is periodic.
    4. Liouville on ℂ² with the compact fundamental domain (the `PsiLiouville` pattern, `latBasis`) gives that `H_ij` is
       constant. ∎
  - **Spike** (`kleinK.py`, exact): the coefficient 3 is forced (`κ = 3` is the unique solution), and the six quadrics
    are short:
    + chart 4:
      * `Q₁₁ = −9/4 X₀X₂ − 9/2 X₁X₈ + 9/2 X₃² + 3/4 X₄X₆`;
      * `Q₁₂ = 3X₀X₃ + 3X₁² − 9/2 X₂X₈ − 3/4 X₄X₇`;
      * `Q₂₂ = X₀² − 3X₁X₂ + 9/2 X₃X₈ + 9/4 X₅X₇`;
    + chart 8:

### 2.3 Model and addition laws
* **Laws by polarization** [Proved]. For `θ = θ_N`, PsiLiouville gives
  `Law_m(θu, θv) = κ_m(u, v)·θ(u + v)` with `κ_m(u, v) = κ·θ_m(u − v)` (`laws_eq`, with `κ` a global constant).
  1. Let `D` and `D′` be `∂₁` in `u` and in `v`. Then `(D + D′)κ_m = 0`.
  2. Differentiate `laws_eq` by `D`, and use `∂₁θ_a = 3(θ_a w̃ − T_a)` and Euler's identity
     `Σ X_a∂_{X_a}Law = 2Law`.
  3. Do the same with `D′`, and average. This gives
     **`κ_m(u, v)·T(u + v, w + w′) = ½ (Σ_a ∂_{X_a}Law_m(θu, θv) T_a(u, w) + Σ_a ∂_{Y_a}Law_m(θu, θv) T_a(v, w′))`**.
  4. The linear `c`-term passes through, because `w ↦ w + ℓ(z)` with `ℓ` linear is additive.

  So **the T-laws are the polarization of the 9 existing laws**:
  - bidegree `(2, 2)` with `T` in the `X`-degree, linear in the fibre;
  - correct and complete exactly where the A-laws are (`laws_complete`).
  - No new law data and no new kernel certificates. In Lean they are *defined* from `lawK` by `MvPolynomial.pderiv`
    and substitution.
  - [Est 0.4–0.7k for `law_eq`/`law_complete`]
* **Model choice** [Design; spike S3 decides].

      `X_cT_l − X_lT_c + ⅓W^{(c)}_{1,l}`, e.g. `X₀T₄ − X₄T₀ + 2X₁² − X₀X₃`.

### M2.1.0 Verdict
* **The P¹¹ model (M-a) is feasible, and much cheaper than feared.** No identity needs the bidegree checker.
  - The new certificate data is tiny:
    + one cubic identity (the Wronskian cocycle, 6 terms);
    + 8 single-copy degree-5 identities for B6 (3.3k cofactor terms in total);
    + one ≤ 5-term identity for `τ₀`, which is optional.
  - Everything else is inherited from the A-block or is semantic.
  - Estimated kernel cost: < 2 CPU-min, ≤ 1 GB per check (P2/B5 format). That is far inside the budget (3.5 GB per
    check, 3 h in total).
* **Five corrections to §2–§5, each with an interface consequence for M2.4a/M2.5:**
  1. The Hilbert function is **not** `(d+1)·9d²`. Only the leading term `9d³` (degree 54) is right (M2.1.1).
  2. `J_V` is not saturated: there is one extra cubic `F` with `(X₀, X₄, X₈)·F ⊆ J_V`. This is harmless after X-only
     slicing.
  3. `J_V` is **not** a local complete intersection at `{X = 0}`, so **X-only slices are mandatory** (M2.1.1).
  4. **B6 cannot use variables as `hilb_y`.** The fourth form must be a linear form; `X₀ + X₄ + X₈` works.
     `VBlock.hilb_y` should therefore be a tuple of linear forms (M2.1.3).
  5. In chart `c` the flows of `T_l` (`l ≠ c`) are **not** quadrics: they are cubics over `X_c²`. `VBlock`'s fibre
     flow field should give `T_c` per chart only (as §2.4 `WT` already sketches), and not a full 12-coordinate quadric
     table (M2.1.5).
* (M-b) is not needed.

### M2.1.1 The ideal `J_V ⊂ ℚ[X₀..X₈, T₀, T₄, T₈]` (`m2_common.py`, `m2_hilb.py`, `m2_excess.py`, `m2_proj.py`)
* **Generators** (15): the 12 A-generators (`gensMv`: 9 quadrics, 3 cubics) and
  - `P04 = X₀T₄ − X₄T₀ + 2X₁² − X₀X₃`
  - `P08 = X₀T₈ − X₈T₀ + X₂X₅ − X₁X₆`
  - `P48 = X₄T₈ − X₈T₄ − 2X₁X₂ + ¼X₅X₇ − ½X₃X₈`

  That is, `P_cl = X_cT_l − X_lT_c + ⅓W^{(c)}_{1l}`, with the chart-0 field for `P04`, `P08` and the chart-4 field
  (`W4C`) for `P48`.
  - On the image they are `θ_flow` (j = 1) in charts 0 and 4, so `θT_model` needs no certificate.
  - All 15 vanish at random image points [Numerical].
  - They are weight-homogeneous for μ₁₀, with `wt T_c = wt X_c + 3`, i.e. `T₀, T₄, T₈ ↦ 3, 2, 1`.
* **Hilbert functions** (weight-blocked mod-p ranks; a mod-p `HF_J` is an upper bound for the ℚ-value):

  | d | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | `HF(S/J_V)` | 12 | 66 | 218 | 522 | 1032 | 1802 | 2886 |
  | `HF` of the image closure `V` [Numerical] | 12 | 66 | 217 | 519 | 1026 | 1792 | (2871) |
  | M2.md's `(d+1)·9d²` | 18 | 108 | 324 | 720 | 1350 | 2268 | 3528 |

  - `HF_V(d) = 9d³ − (11/2)d² + (15/2)d + 1` fits d = 1…6 exactly [Numerical]. So `deg V = 54`.
  - `HF_J − HF_V = C(d−1, 2) = dim F·ℚ[T]_{d−3}`.
* **The extra cubic** [Proved (exact CA)]:
  `F = X₀X₂X₅ + X₀X₁X₆ − 2X₁X₃X₇ − X₀X₇X₈ + (½X₆X₇ − 2X₁X₃)T₀ + (X₂X₆ − X₁X₇ − X₀X₄)T₄ + (X₀² − 4X₁X₂)T₈`.
  - `F` vanishes identically on the image (exact, sympy, modulo the Mumford relations).
  - `F ∉ J_V`, but `X₀F, X₄F, X₈F ∈ J_V` (exact membership), while `T_cF ∉ J_V`.
  - `HF(J_V + (F)) = HF_V` for d ≤ 6 [Numerical], so `I(V) = J_V + (F)` in low degree.
  - `F` is supported on the irrelevant plane. On an X-only slice, `(X₀, X₄, X₈)` is the unit ideal, so `F` lies in the
    sliced `J_V`. **Do not add `F` to `gens`**: it is not needed, and its `lci_mem` would need multiplier `S_iX_c²`.
* **`V ∩ {T = 0}`** = `A ∩ {W₁₄ = W₁₈ = W4₁₈ = 0}` is a zero-dimensional scheme of degree **54**: `HF = 9, 33, 55,
  54, 54, …` (it contains the origin `e₄`).
* **The projection** `(X₀ : X₄ : X₈) : A → ℙ²` is finite (hilbA, already in Lean) of degree **18**: the fibre has
  `HF = 7, 19, 18, 18, …` [Numerical].
* **Local complete intersection.**
  - *On `{X ≠ 0}`: yes*, by the 12 systems of M2.1.2. At random image points every local system has Jacobian rank 8
    (codimension 8 = 11 − 3), and so do all 15 generators [Numerical].
  - *At `{X = 0}`: no* [Proof sketch + Numerical].
    + At `(0; T)` the 15 generators have Jacobian rank 2 [Numerical]. In the chart `T₀ = 1`, `P04` and `P08` eliminate
      `X₄` and `X₈`. All other generators lie in `𝔪²` of a 9-dimensional regular local ring.
    + A complete intersection of codimension 6 there would have multiplicity ≥ 2⁶ = 64.
    + But near `{X = 0}` the variety `V` is 18 smooth branches (the 18 sheets of the projection, glued along the
      boundary `ℙ²`). Top-dimensional lengths are 1 (`J_V` is smooth at the generic points, which have `X ≠ 0`), so the
      multiplicity is 18 < 64.
    + Hence the slicing forms must be X-only. That is MEV's design (`ℓ_k` on base coordinates), and the M2.5 core must
      copy it.

### M2.1.2 B5 (local CI after X-only slicing) (`m2_b5flow.py`) [Proved (exact CA)]
* **Systems.** `F^V_{i,c} = F_i ++ [the two Plückers through c]`, with multiplier `S_i·X_c`, for i < 4 and
  c ∈ {0, 4, 8}.
  - Here `(F_i, S_i)` are the frozen A-block systems (`LSG.Jac.B56`: `lciF`, `lciS`).
  - That gives 12 systems of 8 generators, and `8 + 3 + 1 = 12` (`lci_len`).
* **The one new identity** is the Wronskian cocycle. The `T`-terms cancel:
  `κ := X₀P48 − X₄P08 + X₈P04 = −2X₀X₁X₂ − X₂X₄X₅ + X₁X₄X₆ + ¼X₀X₅X₇ + 2X₁²X₈ − (3/2)X₀X₃X₈ = −½X₅·Q₁ − X₁·Q₅ − ¾·C₀`.
  - It needs a cubic generator, because `κ ∉ (Q)₃`.
  - It serves all three charts: `X_cP_third ∈ (P, P′) + (κ)`.
* **`lci_mem`**: `S_iX_c·G ∈ (F^V_{i,c})` for all 15 `G`.
  - For the 12 A-generators it is the A-block's `lciA_mem` (renamed along `Fin 9 ↪ Fin 12`), times `X_c`.
  - For the two Plückers through `c` it is trivial.
  - For the third Plücker it follows from `κ` and `lciA_mem`.
  - Cross-check: all 12 memberships `X_cS_iP_third ∈ (F^V_{i,c})` hold by exact linear algebra (62–203 cofactor terms
    each, degree 6). **No new kernel certificate is needed.**
* **`lci_cover`** (X-variables only): `X_v^{e+e′} ∈ J_V + (S_iX_c)` comes from the A-block cover (`e ≤ 7`) and
  `hilbA`'s `X_v^{e′_v} ∈ I_A + (X₀, X₄, X₈)` (`e′ ≤ 4`) via a product of ideals. It is a Lean lemma, with no data.
* Lean estimate: 0.2–0.35k.

### M2.1.3 B6 (Hilbert/Noether certificate) (`m2_b6.py`, `m2_b6lin.py`, `m2_b6tri.py`)
* **Why the y must involve T.** `{X = 0} × ℙ²_T ⊂ V(J_V)`, so the four y must restrict to independent forms on
  `ℙ²_T`. Take `T₀, T₄, T₈`. The fourth y must then avoid the degree-54 scheme `V ∩ {T = 0}`.
* **No single variable works** [Numerical].
  - For every `a`, `HF(I_A + W-quadrics + X_a)` stabilises at a positive value (26, 26, 18, 24, 8, 14, 10, 15, 6 for
    `a = 0…8`).
  - Every `X_a` with `a ≠ 4` vanishes at the origin.
  - No binomial `X₄ + λX_b` (λ = ±1, 2) works either: a residue of degree ≥ 2 always remains.
* **`y = (T₀, T₄, T₈, ℓ)` with `ℓ = X₀ + X₄ + X₈` works** (so does `X₀ + X₄ + X₇`).
  - Substitute `X₄ ↦ −X₀ − X₈`, which is exact modulo `(ℓ)`, and set `T = 0`, under which the Plückers become
    `⅓W`-quadrics.
  - Then `X_v⁵ ∈ sub(I_A + W-quadrics)` for all 8 remaining variables, with exact `fmpq` cofactors: 348–536 terms each,
    3.3k in total, degree 5 [Proved (exact CA)].
  - Pigeonhole over 8 variables gives `hilb_deg = 1 + 8·4 = 33`. That changes only the constant `n₁^N` in
    `card_Std_le`.
* **Interface change.** `hilb_y : Fin (g+1) → Fin N` (variables) becomes linear forms
  `hilb_l : Fin (g+1) → (Fin N → ℂ)`.
  - MEB's `SliceBlock` proofs (`hilb_hom`, `yb`, `hom_mem_span_Std`) go through verbatim with `X (hilb_y a)` replaced by
    `lform (hilb_l a)`. Est. +0.1–0.2k in the M2.5 core.
  - The instance proves "monomials of degree 33 lie in `J_V + (y)`" by `M ≡ sub(M) mod (ℓ)` plus pigeonhole on the
    11 remaining variables (`T` exponents ≥ 1 are already in `(y)`).
* **Kernel cost.** 8 single-copy degree-5 identities with ≈ 400 cofactor terms each. In the P2/B5 format
  (`CheckerP2.evalOd`, identity table of degree 5, `Data/B5Id5`) they are estimated at ≈ 5–10 s and ≤ 1 GB each, by
  comparison with B5C (degree 7, ≈ 6 s, ≤ 0.5 GB). `linear_combination` might also do, but that is unmeasured.
* Lean estimate: 0.25–0.45k plus ≈ 3.3k terms of data.

### M2.1.4 The (3,3) polarization laws (`m2_laws.py`) [Numerical checks; sizes exact]
* **Explicit form.** For `m < 9` and charts `c, c′ ∈ {0, 4, 8}` (81 laws, 12 coordinates each):
  - `X_k`-part: `X_cY_{c′}·Law_{m,k}`;
  - `T_k`-part (k ∈ {0, 4, 8}):
    `Y_{c′}T_c·Law_{m,k} + X_cU_{c′}·Law_{m,k} − (1/6)(Y_{c′}·D^X_cLaw_{m,k} + X_c·D^Y_{c′}Law_{m,k})`,
    where `D^X_cL = Σ_a W^{(c)}_{1a}(X)∂L/∂X_a` (the merged derivative of A1's E1 data, here for all 9 laws and the
    first flow direction).
  - This is M2.md §2.3's formula after `Σ_aX_a∂_{X_a}Law = 2Law`. Mathlib has the weighted Euler identity
    (`IsWeightedHomogeneous.sum_weight_X_mul_pderiv`).
* **Checks at random image points** [Numerical, mod p]:
  - all 81 outputs are nonzero, pairwise proportional, and lie on `V`;
  - the law is commutative and associative;
  - `P + [−1]P ∝ (e₄; 0, 0, 0)`, with `[−1](X; T) = (negP X; −T₀, T₄, −T₈)`;
  - `law(P, (e₄; 0, 0, 0)) ∝ P`;
  - `law(P, (e₄; 0, t, 0)) ∝ (X; T + tX_{0,4,8})`, i.e. the fibre translation;
  - the expanded polynomials agree with the structured evaluation.
* **Sizes** [Proved (exact CA)]:
  - X-parts: 74,115 terms (9 × the 8,235 A-law terms).
  - T-parts: 175,760 terms over 243 coordinates, at most 940 per coordinate. `D^X_cLaw_{m,k}` alone accounts for 24k
    terms.
  - Total ≈ 2.5·10⁵ terms, i.e. 30 × the A-law data.
  - For comparison, the (M-b)-style unsubstituted (2,2) T-laws total 31k terms.
* **Which identities need certificates.**
  - **None**, provided the laws are *defined* in Lean from `lawK` by `pderiv`/`rename` and the W tables, and are never
    stored expanded. Expanded data would mean ≈ 25 × A1's `Data/Laws.lean` elaboration (≈ 15–20 min); do not do this.
  - `law_eq` is semantic. Its ingredients are: `laws_eq` differentiated (`hasFDerivAt_evalSP_left`); `θ_flow` to
    replace `∂₁θ_a` by `3(θ_aw̃ − T_a)` with `T_a = (X_aT_c − ⅓W^{(c)}_{1a})/X_c`; weighted Euler; and
    `(D + D′)θ_m(u − v) = 0`.
  - `law_hom (3,3)` is structural (pderiv lowers the X-degree by one; W is a quadric).
  - `law_complete` comes from the A-block's `law_complete` and `chart_cover`, through the X-parts.
  - Nothing in M2.md's semantic argument fails numerically.

### M2.1.6 Surjectivity (no certificates)

    The slice-level uses (`radical_sl`, the component step) see only `X ≠ 0`, because the slices are X-only.
  - If it is needed, the algebraic fact is "`(X₀, X₄, X₈) : V(I_A) → ℂ³` is onto", and it needs no data:
    + `S/I_A` is a finite `ℂ[X₀, X₄, X₈]`-module (from `hilbA`, the MEB `hom_mem_span_Std` argument);

### M2.1.7 `τ₀` (`m2_tau0.py`)
* **`T₀(0,0) = T₈(0,0) = 0`, free.** `(θ(0); T(0,0))` lies on `V` with `θ(0) = ae₄`. Then `P04` gives `−aT₀ = 0` and

  `T(0,0) = a(0, τ₀, 0)`.

  - Exact identity: `T-law^{m,c,4}(X, T; e₄, 0) − λ_mX_cX_mT_k ∈ J_V` for all `m, c, k`, with 0–10 cofactor terms. The

  - With polarized `law_eq` at `v = 0, w′ = 0`, i.e. `law(P, e_an) = κ(u, 0)·P`, this gives `τ₀θ_k(u) = 0` for all

    `norm_facN`), so it costs more.

### M2.4a.1 Design decisions [Design]
1. **One core, for vector blocks only.**
   - `MultEstV` is for `G_a^{d₀} × G_m^{d₁} × (∏_k B_k^♮ pushed out along M)`, with `B : Fin m → VBlock`.
   - There are no mixed `AbBlock`/`VBlock` products:
     + M2 needs only copies of `A_σ̄^♮`.
     + A mixed `Var` doubles every case split in the core (est. +15–25%).
     + An abelian factor cannot cheaply be faked as a `VBlock` either: its fibre would be a spurious `G_a`.
   - Mixed products, if they are ever needed (M3), are a later generalisation.
2. **The push-out uses MEV's model.**
   - `G = G̃_r / K_M`, with `G̃_r = G_a^{d₀+r} × G_m^{d₁} × ∏ B_k^♮` and `K_M = {(0, −Mv; 0; 0, v)}`.
   - Only **onto** `M` is covered, through the hypothesis `Function.Surjective M.mulVec`. In M2, `M = bᵀ` with
     `b ≠ 0`.
   - This is exactly `LSG.MEV.MultEstVecSurj`. So M2.6 copies `Sec7V`'s push-out bookkeeping (`liftT`, `pushT`,
     `tanG`, `t2_eq`), and M2.5 copies `MainVecPush` (`finrank_map_prA_eq`, `finrank_t2_eq`).
3. **Rank-one fibres**: one `G_a` per block, `w : ℂ`. `A_σ̄^♮` and `E^♮` are of this kind. The full `A^♮` (fibre of
   dimension 2) is not needed.
4. **Semantic lift, linear in `w`**: `T_t(z, w) = θ_{fc t}(z)·w − Φ(z)_t` with `Φ` entire. This builds in
   `∂_w T_t = X_{fc t}` and the fibre translation `T ↦ T + sX_{fc}` (`VBlock.T_add`).
5. **Analytic order**: `OrdGtV` (MEB's form), `P ∉ IGsem`, and invariance stated on the lift (`InvarKV`). So the
   consumer needs no bridge for formal series or for cone scalings.
6. **The obstruction carries the classification and `htil`** (the MEB pattern):
   - the data `(U, t, Γ)`, with `IsRatSub` for the base periods;
   - `T̃′ = tanOfV Γ U t = Γ ⊕ U^⊥ ⊕ perTanId t`, and `K_M ⊆ T̃′`;
   - `exp⁻¹(grp) ⊆ ℚ ⊗ (T̃′ + Ω(G̃))`.
