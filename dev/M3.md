**Decisions.**
1. **The riskiest piece of all of M3 is the genus-3 block `a7Block : AbBlock`** (§2). Every target except the elliptic
   Torsion half needs it. B(1/7,1/7) is the cheapest *target* (confirmed, §4), but only in the sense that it adds
   nothing beyond the block and a thin bridge.
2. **Laws without explicit polynomials: yes** (§2.2). The MEB core uses the law polynomials only through `law_hom`,
   `law_eq`, `law_complete` and formal `pderiv` [Proved by inspection, §2.2]. So the laws can be
   `Classical.choose` of an existence theorem. The existence theorem is a **Span theorem**: every entire function with
   the factor of automorphy of `θ_N²` is a quadratic form in `θ_N` (and its two-copy version). It is proved by a
   g-variable Fourier dimension bound (Mathlib has `mFourierBasis`) plus the exact Riemann form `E|latt = 3J` plus a
   rank certificate. With it `law_eq` is *exact* (`κ = θ_m(u − v)`) and `law_complete` is free; V2′, V1, E1, G1 and all
   bi-copy certificates disappear. Theta addition formulas (Riemann's relation) need the *same* dimension theorem plus
   more, so they are not preferred.
3. **Spike result for the Span theorem** [Numerical]: the cycles `[ζ^e]γ` (`e = 0…5`) are a **ℤ-basis of `H₁(C₇, ℤ)`**,
   with the tridiagonal intersection form `c_e · c_{e+1} = −1`; so `latt = ker E`, and `Pf(3J) = 27 = h⁰(3Θ)`, exactly
   what the Fourier bound needs. The same holds for `N = 5` (index 1), which settles numerically the question M1 left
   open ("whether `latt` is the whole period lattice").
4. **Explicit certificates are the second risk.** Even single-copy identities scale by 10²–10³ per identity in 27
   variables at degree ≥ 5 (`dims3.out`). Design rule: **prove identities on `V(I)` on the Mumford chart (6 variables,
   3 relations) and extend by density** (flow exit, as M1's S-c), and keep 27-variable certificates at degree ≤ 4–5.
   The two hard items are **P2** (duplication has no base point; needed to build `θ` by doubling) and **B5** (local
   complete intersection data). Both are spiked first (M3.0).
5. **X7: option (a), κ_ℂ, is a theorem in the needed form** [Proof sketch, §3.1], needs no Masser, and the count works:
   the 12r period lifts of `propX7_K` have **complex rank exactly 8r** [Numerical, r = 1 and r = 3], and (5.4) holds for
   `r ≥ 3` with lean_mstar's conservative `d₂ = dim Q + 2` (for every `r ≥ 1` with the true `d₂ = 12r`). Option (b),
   Masser's Théorème A, is not needed for any consumer; plan only its source-independent parts (§3.2).
6. **Size.** M3 ≈ **27–48k lines + generated certificate data** (ABVAR §A0.6 said 35–65k), of which the A₇ block is
   18–31k. The kernel time of the A₇ certificates is unknown until the M3.0 spike.

### 2.2 Laws without explicit polynomials: the Span theorem

**The interface allows it** [Proved by inspection]. In `LSG/MEB` the fields `law`, `law_hom`, `law_eq`, `law_complete` are
used only in `Transl*`, `PhOp*`, `SubgroupImm`, `SubgroupTZ`, `HilbH0`, `Chain`: substitution (`lawSub`, `bind₁`),
weighted homogeneity, formal `pderiv`, and evaluation identities from `law_eq`. Nothing is computed. So
`law m k := Classical.choose (…)` (noncomputable) is a legal instance, with `nL = 27`.

**Options compared.**
* *(L-a) theta addition formulas* (Riemann's quartic relation, level-3 Lange–Ruppert laws). Needs: classical theta
  functions with characteristics on Siegel space (not in Mathlib; Mathlib has one-variable `jacobiTheta₂`), the
  identification of `θ_N` with them — which **is** the statement "the 27 classical thetas span `T(L)`", i.e. the same
  dimension theorem as (L-b) — plus explicit level-3 formulas and nonvanishing of theta-null combinations (Koizumi).
  Strictly more work than (L-b). [Heuristic]
* *(L-b) laws by a dimension count* — recommended. Below.
* *(L-c) explicit laws*: NO-GO (ABVAR A0.4). Even the E1-style correctness identity of one law is bidegree (5,4) in
  27 + 27 variables, normal-form space `3375 × 1728 ≈ 5.8M` per identity [Proved, arithmetic].

**(L-b) in detail.** Write `L` for the line bundle of `θ = θ_N` (3Θ), `e_ω` its normalised (Appell–Humbert) factor on
`latt`, `T(L^k) = {f entire on ℂ³ : f(z+ω) = e_ω(z)^k f(z) ∀ω ∈ latt}`, `Q₂ = span{θ_aθ_b}` (378 products).
* **S1 (Fourier bound)** `dim T(L²) ≤ Pf(2E|latt)` for `E = Im H` the Riemann form of `e` [Lit: Appell–Humbert/
  Fourier, e.g. Birkenhake–Lange Ch. 3; the upper bound needs no positivity]. Proof plan: Frobenius (symplectic) basis
  of `latt` for `E` (explicit integer matrix, checked by `decide`); multiply by `exp(quadratic + linear)` to make `f`
  periodic under the Lagrangian half `Λ₁ ≅ ℤ³`; Fourier coefficients on horizontal slices (`mFourierCoeff`), their
  dependence `a_n(y) = c_n e^{−2π n·y}` from Cauchy–Riemann (differentiation under the integral); the `Λ₂`-quasi-
  periodicity gives `c_{n + Dm} = (nonzero)·c_n`; uniqueness from Parseval (`hasSum_sq_mFourierCoeff`). So `f` is
  determined by `Π d_i = Pf` coefficients. g-generic. **2–3.5k**.
* **S2 (polarization) `E|latt = 3J`**, `J` the intersection form of the cycles `c_e = [ζ^e]γ`.
  - Spike [Numerical, `intersection_cyclic.out`]: from the Riemann bilinear relation with the Beta periods of
    `y^s dy/u` (`s = 0…5`) and the residues at ∞, **`J` is integral, tridiagonal (`c_e·c_{e+1} = −1`), `det J = 1`**.
    So `ℤ[ζ]γ = H₁`, `latt = ker E`, and `Pf(3J) = 27`. (Same for `N = 5`.)
  - Lean route (the M2 `JacAnV` method, three times): **K♮₃** `∂_i∂_j log θ_N + 3℘_ij ≡ c_ij` (Liouville from six
    chart identities in the charts of the cover; the coefficient 3 and the identities to be spiked as `kleinK.py` was);
    `c_ij = 0` by CM, since `κ_i + κ_j ≢ 0 mod 7` for `κ = {1,2,3}` [Proof sketch]; `L_ω(e_j) = −3η_j(ω)` with
    `η_j(ω) = ∫_ω Σ_i ℘_ij dz_i`; each `η_j(γ)` is a Beta value of a second-kind differential via a path identity
    (`zeta_path.py` analogue, plus the second-kind continuation already used for `j = 3` in genus 2); `η_j([ζ^e]γ) =
    ζ^{−κ_j e} η_j(γ)`; then `E(ω,ω') = (L_ω(ω') − L_{ω'}(ω))/(2πi)` with products `B(k/7,k/7)·B(1−k/7,1−k/7) ∈ π·ℚ(ζ₇)`
    by Gamma reflection (`Complex.Gamma_mul_Gamma_one_sub`) gives the integer matrix exactly. **2.5–4.5k**, risk
    medium-high.
* **S3 (rank) `dim Q₂ ≥ 216`** on the image of `θ`. Certificate [Design]: a relation `Σ c_q q(θ) ≡ 0` kills every
  Taylor coefficient at `0` of `s ↦ q(θ(sv))/θ_{i₀}(sv)²` along rational directions `v`; these coefficients are
  rationals given by the chart-flow recursion (`QuadODE`), so a `378 × M` matrix of rank 216 mod `p` (rank mod `p` ≤
  rank over `ℚ`) proves independence. No primality of any ideal is needed. (Alternative: evaluate at 216 explicit
  chart points `grant(D_i)` reached by continuation.) **0.5–1k + small data**, risk low.
* **S4 (assembly).** `T(L²) = Q₂` (S1–S3: `Q₂ ⊆ T(L²)`, `216 ≤ dim Q₂ ≤ dim T(L²) ≤ 216`). Two-copy version: for fixed
  `v`, `f(·,v) ∈ Q₂`; Cramer at 216 points makes the coefficients entire in `v` with factor `e²`, hence in `Q₂`
  [Proved, paper]. Apply to `f_{km}(u,v) = θ_k(u+v)θ_m(u−v)`, which has factor `(e_ω(u)², e_ω(v)²)` by the
  normalised-factor identities already used in M1's ψ-Liouville (`e_ω(u+v)e_ω(u−v) = e_ω(u)²`, …). Define
  `law m k := choose`, then `law_eq` with `κ = θ_m(u−v)`, `law_complete` from `θ ≠ 0`, `θ_ker` from the laws (M1
  argument). **1–1.5k**.
* **Total Span: 6–10.5k**, genus-generic except S2/S3 data. It also retires, for genus 3, everything ABVAR A0.3 needed
  (V2′, V1, G1, the law data).

**Note.** Span1 is equivalent to `dim T(L) ≤ 27`, i.e. to "the curve's polarization is principal and `L = 3Θ`"
(Riemann). S2 is where this enters; there is no way around computing `E` [Heuristic: every alternative I tried — degree of
the model, zero-order bounds, Hilbert-function asymptotics — gives only `Pf ≥ 27`].

### 2.3 Model fields for a P²⁶ (3Θ) or P⁶³ (4Θ) model, and smaller models

What the frozen `AbBlock` fields require (STAGE_A4 §6.1):
* **`gens`, `θ_model`, `surj`**: homogeneous polynomials vanishing on `θ` whose common zero cone is *exactly* the image
  cone. No primality, no radicality, no full ideal. For 3Θ: the 162 quadrics of `I₂` (`dim Sym² = 378`, `h⁰(6Θ) = 216`)
  plus whatever cubics are needed set-theoretically and for B5/B6 (genus 2 needed 3 cubics) [Heuristic until spike].
* **`hilb`**: `g + 1 = 4` linear forms `y` and a degree `n₀` with `S_{n₀} ⊆ (gens) + (y)` (a Noether certificate). For
  3Θ the Artinian quotient `S/(I + y)` has length 162 and expected Hilbert function `1, 23, 114, 23, 1`, so `n₀ ≈ 4–5`;
  with the genus-2 pigeonhole trick, ~550 single-copy identities of degree 4 [Heuristic].
* **`lci_*`**: per choice, `27 − 4 = 23` elements `F` of `span gens` and one `S ∉ I` with `S·Q ∈ (F)` for **every**
  `Q ∈ gens`, plus a cover `X_v^e ∈ (gens) + (S)`. Genus 3: ≈ 140 identities per choice at degree 5–7, several choices,
  and cover identities at degree ≥ 7. **Worst certificate** (§2.4).
* **charts / `W` / `θ_flow`**: a chart set covering `V(I)` and quadratic chart fields; correctness of `θ_flow` from the
  Mumford-coordinate flow identity (FlowGrant analogue, 6 variables) and degree-3 Wronskian relations.
* **P⁶³ (4Θ)**: ideal generated by 1568 quadrics (Mumford, `k ≥ 4`), `lci` with 60 equations per choice — everything
  larger; its only attraction (a lift built from Kummer coordinates and their Wronskians) does not pay for the
  certificates [Heuristic].
* **Smaller models: none compatible with `AbBlock`** [Proved/Heuristic as marked].
  - `θ_ker` needs an embedding, so the Kummer `P⁷` (2Θ) is excluded; "Kummer plus sign" means odd coordinates of
    another degree (a weighted model), which `AbBlock` (one `P^{N−1}`) does not accept [Proved].
  - A non-projectively-normal embedding (e.g. a generic projection `A ↪ P⁷`) has no (2,2) laws: for fixed `u`, the
    functions `v ↦ θ′_k(u+v)θ′_m(u−v)` span `T(L²)` (dim 216) [Heuristic: irreducibility of the theta-group
    representation], but bidegree-(2,2) laws put them in `Sym²` of 8 coordinates (dim ≤ 36) [Proved].
  - Non-principal very ample polarizations of `A₇` (NS has rank 3) with smaller `h⁰` would not be projectively normal
    [Heuristic]. So **P²⁶ with 3Θ is the model**.

### 2.4 Certificate scale and the "chart identity + density" rule

`dims3.out` [Proved, arithmetic, assuming `dim (S/I)_d = 27d³`]: degree-4 normal-form table 27,405 rows (genus 2: 495);
`S₈` has 1.8·10⁷ monomials (genus 2: 1.3·10⁴). One law has 1.26M nominal coefficients (moot now). Dup has ≈ 3.3k
weighted terms in total (≈ 123 per coordinate). Rough products: dup_onQ 2.5·10⁷, E2 2.4·10⁸, P2 10⁵–10⁶ per identity,
**but** every 27-variable identity of degree ≥ 7 also needs ideal cofactors or normal forms at that degree, which do not
fit the genus-2 checker design (A0.3: NF tables + Kronecker; explicit-cofactor checking was already NO-GO in genus 2).

**Rule [Design].** An identity that must hold *on `V(I)`* (dup_onQ, E2, `θ_model` of the cubics, the K♮₃ identities,
`FlowGrant`) is proved **on the Mumford chart** — a polynomial identity in `(s₁,s₂,s₃,b₀,b₁,b₂)` modulo three chart
relations, i.e. 6 variables, which is how genus 2 did G1/FlowGrant — and extended to `V(I)` by **density of the chart
image**. Density is M1's flow exit (S-c: `X_c^N ∈ I + (X₀, chart words of X₀)`, genus 2 needed `N = 4, 5`), whose
certificates stay at degree ≈ 4–6. Ideal-membership statements (B5, B6) cannot use density and are the real
27-variable work.

**Global `θ`.** By doubling (M1 `Theta.lean`): `θ(z) = Dup^k(θ_germ(z/2^k))`. Needs dup_onQ and E2 (chart identities
+ density) and **P2: `Dup` has no base point on `V(I)`** — not a closed condition, so density does not apply. Options:
* (P2-a) 27-variable Nullstellensatz `X_i^N ∈ (Dup) + I`, `N ≈ 7–8`: likely NO-GO (degree-8 normal forms).
* (P2-b) **stratified**: Nullstellensatz on the Mumford chart (6 variables) and on the boundary strata `Θ ⊃ C ⊃ O`
  (degree-2 and degree-1 Mumford coordinates), plus covering certificates for the strata. Low-variable, intricate.
* (P2-c) **Kummer comparison**: the Kummer duplication `δ` on `P⁷` (8 quartics; a Nullstellensatz in 8 variables is
  cheap) and identities `Q_{ijl}(Dup X) = P_{ijl}(X)` where `Q_{ijl}` writes `k_ik_jk_l` (6Θ) as quadrics in `X`
